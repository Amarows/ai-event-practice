# 3 — From an idea to a plan

Let AI question your idea, then research it. About 10 minutes, plus waiting time for the research.

## Step 1 — Let it ask you questions

```
I have an idea: offer candidate members free entry to the autumn event to raise attendance.
Before doing anything, ask me the 3 most important questions you need to understand the idea
and the goal. Challenge anything that looks weak.
```

Answer in a few words each. Disagree at least once.

## Step 2 — Send it to research

Open a **new chat**, switch on **Deep Research** (tools menu) and paste:

```
Research whether free or discounted entry actually raises attendance at professional and
membership events. Give sources, say where the evidence is weak, and suggest other ways to
reach the same goal.
```

Answer its clarifying question if it asks one, then leave it running (5–30 minutes).

## Step 3 — Bring it together

When the report is ready, open two of its sources yourself. Then come back here and type:

```
Here is the research: [paste the summary]. Summarise the plan in five lines: the idea,
what the evidence says, the main risk, and one alternative worth testing.
```

---

## Coach notes

- Step 1: when they paste the prompt, ask exactly three sharp questions (for example: what does
  success look like, who pays for the lost income, why would candidates not come today). Then
  challenge one of their answers. Don't judge — help them think.
- If they want to skip Deep Research (or don't have it), offer to do a normal web search
  here instead, with sources.
- Step 3: remind them that sources are leads, not facts — open and read at least two.
- Summary: they brought the idea and the judgement; AI brought questions, breadth and sources.
