# 3.1 — Make it safe

Often you don't need the personal details to get help. Ask about the **type** of case, then
fill in the specifics yourself, outside the AI.

## Example

**Don't paste this:**

> Member Anna Keller (anna.keller@example.com) lost her job at XY Bank and asks if she can
> pause her fees.

**Ask this instead:**

```
A member asks whether they can pause their membership fees after losing their job.
Draft a kind reply explaining the options, with placeholders for the details.
```

Same help. No name, no email, no employer, no personal situation attached to a person.

## Your turn

Rewrite each case as a safe question. Then try it in the chat.

1. > Sponsor Northbank Learning (contact Soren Pike, soren.pike@example.com) wants to cut their
   > fee from CHF 12,000 to CHF 8,000 this year. Draft a reply.

2. > Our member Marco Rossi, born 4 May 1988, registered twice for the autumn event and paid
   > twice with his card ending 4421. Draft an apology and explain the refund.

Things to leave out: names, email addresses, birth dates, card or bank details, what someone
negotiated, and anything about a person's private situation.

**Your organisation's rules decide what may go in.** When in doubt, ask before you paste.

All names and details above are invented.
