# 4.4 — Translate with the right tone

**Situation:** an English invitation needs a German version for Swiss members.

## Text (fictional)

> Join us on Thursday 22 October for an evening on better decisions from information.
> It's free for members, places are limited to 60, and registration closes on Tuesday
> 20 October at 17:00. We look forward to seeing you there!

## Try this

```
Translate this invitation into German for members in Switzerland.
Use the formal "Sie", Swiss spelling (ss, not ß) and Swiss date format.
Keep it warm but not exaggerated.
Then list any wording choices you made that I should check.
```

Then ask: `Translate your German version back into English, word for word.`
Does it still say the same thing?

## Check

- Dates and times unchanged?
- "ss" instead of "ß"?
- Tone: would you be happy to sign it?
- For anything important, have a native speaker read it.
