# 4.2 — Pick newsletter items

**Situation:** the next newsletter has room for three items. You have six candidates.
AI can rank them — but only by criteria you give it.

## Candidates (fictional)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Autumn member event | 22 Oct · 42 of 60 places taken |
| Winter networking evening | 3 Dec · 12 of 80 places taken |
| Career morning | 16 Jan · registration opens 1 Nov |
| New sponsor welcome | Sponsor D joins this month |
| Volunteer call | 2 volunteers needed for the winter evening |
| Photo gallery | Pictures from the summer event are online |

## Try this

```
Pick three items for our next newsletter from this list.
Criteria, in order:
1. Events that need registrations most urgently.
2. Anything with a deadline in the next six weeks.
3. Keep one lighter item for variety.
Show your ranking in a table with one sentence of reasoning per item.
```

Then change one criterion and ask again. Did the ranking change the way you expected?

## Check

- Does the ranking follow **your** criteria, or its own?
- Are the numbers copied correctly?

All details are invented.
