# 4.3 — Answer a member question

**Situation:** a member writes:

> Can I bring a colleague who isn't a member to the winter networking evening?
> And is there still space?

The facts are in **2.2** (events calendar).

## Try this

```
Using only the events calendar in file 2.2, draft a short, friendly reply to this member
question: "Can I bring a colleague who isn't a member to the winter networking evening?
And is there still space?"
If something is not in the calendar, say so in the reply instead of guessing.
```

## Check

- Guests: allowed? What does it cost? (see 2.2)
- Deadline: right date and time?
- Space: the calendar gives total places, not how many are left. Did it guess, or say it doesn't know?

The last point is the real test: a good answer admits what it doesn't know.
