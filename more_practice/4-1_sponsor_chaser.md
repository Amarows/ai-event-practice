# 4.1 — Chase a late sponsor

**Situation:** it's 18 September. Some deliverables for the autumn event are late. You want
friendly reminders — and you will send them yourself.

## Deadline list (fictional)

| What | From | Due | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brochure advert | Sponsor A | 15 Sep | not received |
| Speaker bio and photo | Speaker B | 18 Sep | received |
| Final guest numbers | Venue | 25 Sep | not yet due |
| Logo for the slides | Sponsor C | 10 Sep | not received |

## Try this

```
Here is my deadline list. Today is 18 September.
Find what is overdue. For each overdue item, draft a short, friendly reminder email
(max 80 words) that states the item, the original date and a new date one week from today.
I will review and send them myself.
```

## Check before you send

- Did it pick exactly the overdue items — and not the one that isn't due yet?
- Are the dates right?
- Does it promise anything you didn't agree to?

All names and details are invented.
