# 4 — Make it safe

Ask for help without sharing personal details. About 5 minutes. 2 steps.

**The idea:** whatever you paste goes into the chat and leaves your organisation. Often you
don't need the details at all: ask about the **type** of case, get a draft with placeholders,
and fill in the specifics yourself, outside the AI.

## Step 1 of 2 — See the difference

**Why:** the same help, without the personal details.

**Instead of:**

> Member Anna Keller (anna.keller@example.com) lost her job at XY Bank and asks if she can pause
> her fees.

**Ask:**

```
A member asks whether they can pause their membership fees after losing their job.
Draft a kind reply explaining the options, with placeholders for the details.
```

**Notice:** no name, no email, no employer — and the reply is just as useful.

## Step 2 of 2 — Your turn

**Why:** spotting what to leave out is the skill. Here is a case as a colleague might paste it
(invented details). Rewrite it as a safe question and send your version:

> Sponsor Northbank Learning (contact Soren Pike, soren.pike@example.com) wants to cut their fee
> from CHF 12,000 to CHF 8,000 this year. Draft a reply.

**Notice:** what did you remove, and why?

All names and details are invented.

---

## Coach notes

- Step 1: show both the "Instead of" text (with the name and email) and the safe version, so the
  difference is visible. The details are invented, so showing them is safe.
- Step 2: show the case **exactly as written, with all its personal details**. Do not anonymise
  it yourself; rewriting it is the exercise. **Never show or hint at a safe version before they
  have written their own.**
- What should be gone: the sponsor's name, the contact person, the email, the exact amounts
  (what someone negotiated). If something identifying is left, point it out kindly and let them
  fix it. When their version is safe, answer it normally.
- Always add: their organisation's rules decide what may go in. When in doubt, ask before pasting.
- What they learned: ask about the type of case; add the specifics yourself.
