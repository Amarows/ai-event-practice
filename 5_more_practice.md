# 5 — More practice

Four short tasks from everyday event work. Pick one. About 5 minutes each.

## A — Chase late deliverables

Today is 18 September. The list:

| What | From | Due | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brochure advert | Sponsor A | 15 Sep | not received |
| Speaker bio and photo | Speaker B | 18 Sep | received |
| Final guest numbers | Venue | 25 Sep | not yet due |
| Logo for the slides | Sponsor C | 10 Sep | not received |

```
Using the list above: find what is overdue. For each overdue item, draft a short, friendly
reminder (max 80 words) with the item, the original date and a new date one week from today.
I will review and send them myself.
```

## B — Pick newsletter items

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Autumn member event | 22 Oct · 42 of 60 places taken |
| Winter networking evening | 3 Dec · 12 of 80 places taken |
| Career morning | 16 Jan · registration opens 1 Nov |
| New sponsor welcome | Sponsor D joins this month |
| Volunteer call | 2 volunteers needed for the winter evening |
| Photo gallery | Pictures from the summer event are online |

```
Pick three items for our next newsletter from the list above. Criteria, in order:
1. events that need registrations most urgently, 2. anything with a deadline in the next
six weeks, 3. one lighter item for variety. Show a table with one sentence of reasoning each.
```

Then change one criterion and ask again.

## C — Answer a member question

A member asks: *"Can I bring a colleague who isn't a member to the winter networking evening?
And is there still space?"*

```
Using only the events calendar from this practice, draft a short, friendly reply to the
member question above. If something isn't in the calendar, say so instead of guessing.
```

## D — Translate with the right tone

> Join us on Thursday 22 October for an evening on better decisions from information. It's free
> for members, places are limited to 60, and registration closes on Tuesday 20 October at 17:00.
> We look forward to seeing you there!

```
Translate this invitation into German for members in Switzerland. Formal "Sie", Swiss spelling
(ss, not ß), Swiss date format, warm but not exaggerated. Then list the wording choices I
should check.
```

---

## Coach notes

- Show the menu A–D first, then only the chosen task.
- A: correct answer is two overdue items — the brochure advert (Sponsor A) and the logo
  (Sponsor C). The venue item is not yet due; the speaker item is received. New date: 25 September.
  Ask them to check whether it picked exactly these.
- B: ask whether the ranking followed **their** criteria or its own, and whether numbers were
  copied correctly. The winter evening (12 of 80) is the most urgent by criterion 1.
- C: open the events calendar (2_events_calendar.md in this repository) if it isn't in the chat
  yet. Facts: guests allowed, one per member, CHF 25; register by Monday 30 November, 12:00.
  The calendar gives total places (80), **not** how many are left — a good reply says it doesn't
  know. That's the real test.
- D: suggest asking for a word-for-word back-translation to check the meaning, and having a
  native speaker read anything important.
