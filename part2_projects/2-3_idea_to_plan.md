# 2.3 — From an idea to a plan

Use this inside the Project from **2.1**, so ChatGPT already knows the events.
Two roles in one conversation: a **tutor** that asks and challenges, and a **researcher** that
searches by meaning.

## Step 1 — Let it ask you questions

```
I have an idea: offer candidate members free entry to the autumn event to raise attendance.
Before doing anything, ask me the 3 most important questions you need to understand the idea
and the goal. Challenge anything that looks weak.
```

Answer in a few words each. Push back at least once if you disagree.

## Step 2 — Send it to research

Open a **new chat** in the same Project. Switch on **Deep Research** (in the tools menu) and paste:

```
Research whether free or discounted entry actually raises attendance at professional and
membership events. Use the context of this project. Give sources, say where the evidence is
weak, and suggest other ways to reach the same goal.
```

If it asks a clarifying question, answer it. Then leave it running — it can take 5–30 minutes.
Carry on with something else meanwhile.

## Step 3 — Bring it together

When the report is ready, open two of its sources yourself. Sources are leads, not facts.
Then, back in the Step 1 chat:

```
Here is the research: [paste the summary]. Summarise the plan in five lines: the idea,
what the evidence says, the main risk, and one alternative worth testing.
```

You brought the idea and the judgement. AI brought questions, breadth and sources.
